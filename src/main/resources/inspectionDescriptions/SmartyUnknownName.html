Reports tags and modifiers that Smarty would not know: neither built in, nor a plugin of the project (function.name.php, block.name.php, modifier.name.php, $smarty->registerPlugin(...)), nor a template function ({function name="x"} in the template or its layouts) or a class imported with {use class="..."}. With the PHP plugin, PHP functions count as known modifiers.

Off by default: plugins in directories outside the project, or registered in ways that cannot be seen in the code, are reported too.
